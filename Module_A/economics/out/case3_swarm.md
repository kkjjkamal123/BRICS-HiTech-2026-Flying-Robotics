# Economic efficiency - Case 3 - Swarm early detection of Red Palm Weevil (500 ha date-palm plantation)

**Customer / user:** Date-palm plantation operator (UAE consortium)  
**Purpose:** Find infested palms early, before visible damage, so they can be treated and saved  
**Horizon:** 5 years, **discount rate:** 18.0% (key rate + risk premium)

## 1. Cost and benefit items

| No. | Cost/benefit item | Description | Amount / year | Comment |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Development / capital cost (one-time) | Swarm of 4 UAVs with thermal / multispectral sensors | 2 400 000 RUB |  |
| 2 | Development / capital cost (one-time) | Swarm control + detection software | 1 500 000 RUB |  |
| 3 | Development / capital cost (one-time) | Ground station, network, chargers | 300 000 RUB |  |
| 4 | Operating expense (per year) | Operator (1 FTE for the swarm) | 1 200 000 RUB |  |
| 5 | Operating expense (per year) | Maintenance, batteries, insurance | 400 000 RUB |  |
| 6 | Benefit / saving (per year) | Avoided crop losses: 1% of plantation revenue (75 000 palms x 6 000 RUB) | 4 500 000 RUB |  |
| 7 | Benefit / saving (per year) | Manual tree-by-tree inspection replaced | 1 200 000 RUB |  |

**Totals:** CAPEX 4 200 000 RUB · OPEX 1 600 000 RUB/yr · Benefits 5 700 000 RUB/yr (year-1 basis)

## 3. Efficiency indicators

| Indicator | Value | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Payback period (simple) | **1.59 years** | investment returned |
| Discounted payback | 1.92 years | with time value of money |
| ROI (5 yr) | **323.3%** | (total net benefit - investment) / investment |
| NPV @ 18.0% | **6 425 970 RUB** | > 0 = creates value |
| IRR | **66.9%** | > discount rate = feasible |
| Profitability index | 2.53 | PV of inflows / investment (>1 good) |
| Break-even benefits | 59.6% of plan | NPV = 0 if benefits fall to this level |

| Solution | Cost per year | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Manual inspection teams | 2 400 000 RUB | 1 palm every few minutes, infestation found too late |
| Single UAV | 900 000 RUB | 4x slower coverage, single point of failure |
| **Our UAV system** | 1 600 000 RUB (+ CAPEX 4 200 000 RUB once) | swarm covers the plantation 4x faster and survives one drone failure |

## 6. Conclusion

The project is **ECONOMICALLY FEASIBLE**: NPV 6 425 970 RUB > 0, IRR 66.9% vs discount rate 18.0%, payback 1.59 years.
It stays feasible as long as benefits are above 59.6% of the plan.

**Key factors:**
- infestation rate
- value of a saved palm
- share detected early

**Recommendations:**
- combine with pheromone traps data
- weekly survey during the breeding season

**Social effects:**
- protects farmers' income
- less broad-spectrum insecticide
- food security
