# Economic efficiency - Case 2 - Automatic weed mapping for spot spraying (2 000 ha farm)

**Customer / user:** Agro-holding / farm (agronomy service)  
**Purpose:** Count and map weeds automatically to spray herbicide only where needed  
**Horizon:** 5 years, **discount rate:** 18.0% (key rate + risk premium)

## 1. Cost and benefit items

| No. | Cost/benefit item | Description | Amount / year | Comment |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Development / capital cost (one-time) | UAV with multispectral + RGB camera | 1 500 000 RUB |  |
| 2 | Development / capital cost (one-time) | Weed detection software + GIS integration | 800 000 RUB |  |
| 3 | Development / capital cost (one-time) | Training of agronomists / operator | 100 000 RUB |  |
| 4 | Operating expense (per year) | Operator (seasonal) incl. taxes | 450 000 RUB |  |
| 5 | Operating expense (per year) | Maintenance, batteries, processing compute | 250 000 RUB |  |
| 6 | Benefit / saving (per year) | Herbicide saving: 2 000 ha x 2 500 RUB/ha x 30% (spot spraying) | 1 500 000 RUB |  |
| 7 | Benefit / saving (per year) | Agronomist field-scouting time saved | 600 000 RUB |  |
| 8 | Benefit / saving (per year) | Yield gain 1% (2 000 ha x 4 t/ha x 15 000 RUB/t x 1%) | 1 200 000 RUB |  |

**Totals:** CAPEX 2 400 000 RUB · OPEX 700 000 RUB/yr · Benefits 3 300 000 RUB/yr (year-1 basis)

## 3. Efficiency indicators

| Indicator | Value | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Payback period (simple) | **1.31 years** | investment returned |
| Discounted payback | 1.57 years | with time value of money |

| Solution | Cost per year | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Manual scouting by agronomists | 1 400 000 RUB | slow, a few % of the field sampled |
| Blanket herbicide spraying | 5 000 000 RUB | chemicals everywhere |
| **Our UAV system** | 700 000 RUB (+ CAPEX 2 400 000 RUB once) | 100% field coverage, weed map in hours |

## 6. Conclusion

The project is **ECONOMICALLY FEASIBLE**: NPV 4 705 810 RUB > 0, IRR 82.7% vs discount rate 18.0%, payback 1.31 years.
It stays feasible as long as benefits are above 50.4% of the plan.

**Key factors:**
- herbicide price
- share of field that can be spot-sprayed
- model accuracy (weed vs crop)

**Recommendations:**
- export maps to the sprayer (prescription maps)
- offer as a service to neighbouring farms

**Social effects:**
- less herbicide in soil and water
- healthier food
- lower carbon footprint
